1:00 to 2:00. Regen Bazaar is a marketplace for impact that has already happened. There are three roles. A creator, which can be an NGO, an informal group or one person, reports work they finished, with evidence. A validator checks the proof. Then a buyer, a person, a company or an AI agent, buys editions of that verified impact. Each purchase pays the creator directly, in the same transaction. Buyers pay for a delivered result, not for a promise. The money goes straight to the people who created it, and that is how more impact gets funded: after the result, not before. Why Web3? Because anyone can check who was paid, how much, and for which verified claim, without trusting us. And a stablecoin can pay a creator who cannot receive a foreign bank transfer.
