How it works

From a plain report to an on-chain sale

1

Report

The creator describes the work in plain words and adds photos or links.

2

Extract

AI pulls out the facts. A published formula computes the Impact Value. AI never scores.

3

Validate

A validator sets the proof level, P0 to P4. Nothing is listed without it.

4

Attest

EAS attestation on-chain, metadata on IPFS. One report, one network.

5

Buy

Editions are minted to the buyer. One transaction splits the payment.

Impact Value is Regen Bazaar’s own relative index (methodology v0.2), not a certification or a carbon credit.

2:00 to 2:55. Here is how it works, in five steps. One. The creator writes what they did in plain words, and adds photos or links. Two. An AI model reads the text and pulls out the facts: quantities, units, place. The AI never gives the score. A published, versioned formula turns those facts into one number, the Impact Value. It is our own relative index. It is not a carbon credit and not a certification. Three. A validator sets the proof level, from P0 to P4, and approves. Nothing is listed without this step. Four. The claim is attested on-chain with EAS attestations, and the metadata is pinned to IPFS. One report is listed on exactly one network, so the same work cannot be sold twice.
