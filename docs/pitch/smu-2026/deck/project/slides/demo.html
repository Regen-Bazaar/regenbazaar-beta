The whole flow in three minutes

[Video from smu-demo, due Sat 21.11]

4:00 to 7:00. Click play. Narrate over the silent video. 0:00 This is a recorded run on Arbitrum Sepolia, a test network. Three people: a creator, a validator and a buyer. ~0:10 A creator writes what they did, in plain words, and adds a link to the proof. While they type, the app shows the Impact Value it will compute. They submit. ~0:55 Now the validator. On one side, what the AI read from the report. On the other, the creator's own numbers. The validator checks the proof, sets proof level P2, and approves. That writes an EAS attestation on-chain and pins the metadata to IPFS. ~1:45 Here is the listing: the Impact Value with its full breakdown, the price that comes from it, and the split, 97.5 percent to the creator and 2.5 to the platform. The buyer clicks Buy and confirms in the wallet. In one transaction the edition is minted to the buyer and the creator is paid. Nobody holds the money in between. ~2:30 Last, the buyer retires one edition in their own name. It is burned, permanently, and the chain records who retired it.
