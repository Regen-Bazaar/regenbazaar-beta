# Impact Value methodology v0.2 (Community layer)

Status: in development, not live. Reports scored before v0.2 keep their v0.1 score and version.

Impact Value (IV) is Regen Bazaar's own relative index of impact. It is not a certification, not a carbon
or biodiversity credit, and it does not claim any emissions result for a buyer. It is built for small NGOs
and informal groups ("community layer"); projects certified by a registry can declare their serial number,
which prevents double claiming.

## Two streams that meet only in the price

1. **How much impact.** Report text → an AI model lists the actions and numbers it states (it never assigns
   value) → quantities are converted to physical units → domain scores → one IV.
2. **How sure we are.** Proof links and media → AI checks raise flags (dates, place, numbers, repeats) → a
   human validator sets the proof level P0–P4.

## Units

| Action | Converted to | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Trees, mangroves | hectares per monitored year, from declared area or planting density | IPCC 2019 Refinement, Bernal et al. 2018 |
| Hectares restored | hectares per monitored year | as above |
| Tonnes, grams, pounds | kg | SI |
| m², acres, rai | ha | SI; 1 rai = 0.16 ha |
| Litres of drinking water | person-days (5.5 L per adult per day, 5% deduction on defaults) | Gold Standard Safe Drinking Water Supply v2.0 |
| kWh of solar or wind | tCO2e with the country grid factor | IFI Default Grid Factors v3.0 |
| Schools | m² of classroom space | IRIS+ PI7268 |

A quantity whose unit cannot be converted scores 0 and is flagged for the reviewer.

## Formula

```
domain score = Σ units × AW × SM × ESM × S        (per domain)
IV           = Σ domain score × k                  (k: published domain coefficient)
```

- **AW**, weight per physical unit. Environment is in tCO2e-equivalent points (1 point = 1 tCO2e). Every
  weight has a justification card in [`cards/`](cards/README.md) with its source status: sourced, derived
  (our arithmetic on a cited table) or assumption (needs check).
- **SM**, area factor for area-based ecosystem actions: 0.75 under 0.5 ha, 0.9 from 0.5 ha, 1.0 from 1 ha or
  when the site joins existing habitat. Computed on the report's total area for that action. Other actions 1.0.
  Direction from Haddad et al. 2015 (habitat fragmentation); thresholds need checking.
- **ESM**, environmental sensitivity, 1.0 to 1.3, environment only. Suggested from open data layers at the
  site coordinates and confirmed by a validator.
- **S**, survival: coral 0.65 (Boström-Einarsson et al. 2020); mangroves by measured surviving share, default
  0.72 (proxy from Bourgeois et al. 2024).
- **k**, domain coefficients: 1.0 for all six domains until the cost survey with pilot groups.

Screens show the domain score and physical units first (for example "27.7 tCO2e/yr, 1.2 ha of mangroves");
the single IV is used for price, staking and the overall ranking.

## Rules inside one report

- Lines of the same action are summed before scoring; splitting text into lines cannot change the score.
- Area and tree count of the same planting: area counts, the count is evidence.
- tCO2e counts only with a registry serial; then tree and area rows of that report are evidence only.
- Recycled plastic adds only its carbon co-benefit when collection is also reported.
- Workshops score 0 when participants are counted. Meals and family support for the same families: the higher
  one counts. Patients and vaccinations or kits: the validator decides.
- Volunteers are an input and score 0; they are shown as context.
- Wildlife releases need justification and post-release monitoring (IUCN/SSC 2013).
- One merged action is capped at 1,000,000 input units. Carbon rows credit at most 20 monitored years.

## Proof levels

| Level | What is needed | Price factor P (pilot hypothesis) |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | text only | not listed |
| P1 | at least one public link within the period that matches the claim | 0.6 |
| P2 | photo or video with date and place, or two independent public traces; clean AI flags | 0.8 |
| P3 | a second party confirms, or 3+ earlier verified reports | 0.9 |
| P4 | third-party measurement, instrument or registry record | 1.0 |

AI checks only raise flags. The level is set by a person and never changes IV.

## Price

Planned formula (pending owner approval): price in USD = IV × rate × P × C, split across editions. C is the
former complexity multiplier (1.0 to 1.4, same five questions), moved out of IV because difficulty is a cost,
not an outcome.

## Removed from v0.1

- **TBV** (time bonus): a longer reporting period is not more impact. Carbon is credited per monitored year.
- **PIM** (population density): reach is counted directly (people, students, patients).
- **ACDM** (complexity): moved from IV to price.

## Alignment

Definitions are mapped to IRIS+ metric IDs (IRIS+ by the GIIN, cited with attribution) and structured around
the Impact Management Project's Five Dimensions. SDG tags mean "contributes to"; SDG indicators are
country-level.
