# Methodology changelog

## v0.2 (Community layer), live since 2026-09-28

Tables version `v0.2-community-2026-09`. Reports scored before keep their v0.1 score (`v0.1-seed-2026-06`) and their minted IV.
v0.1 and v0.2 numbers are on different scales and should not be compared directly.

| Item | v0.1 | v0.2 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weights | 34 seed numbers without records | same 34 actions, each with a justification card and source status | every number must be checkable |
| Units | raw counts in any unit | physical units (ha·yr, kg, person-days, tCO2e) | "3,000 mangroves" and "1.5 ha of mangroves" now score the same |
| SM | 1.0 to 2.0 by quantity, per line | area factor 0.75 to 1.0 for small isolated patches, on the report total | bigger is not better per unit; fragmentation is (Haddad 2015); cleanups scale linearly (Burt 2020) |
| TBV | 1 + 0.1 × years | removed | a longer period is not more impact |
| ESM | region from a list, 1.0 to 1.5, self-declared | 1.0 to 1.3 from open data layers, confirmed by a validator | narrower and checkable |
| PIM | 1.0 to 1.8 by density | removed | reach is counted directly |
| ACDM | 1.0 to 1.42 in IV | moved to price | difficulty is a cost |
| Survival S | none | coral 0.65, mangroves by surviving share | not every planted unit survives |
| Domain coefficients k | implicit | six published numbers, all 1.0; categories not compared yet | makes the cross-domain value judgement explicit |
| Proof | did not affect anything | P0–P4, set by a person; affects listing and price only | confidence is separate from quantity |
| Duplicates in one report | not handled | merge lines; area vs trees; registry carbon; workshops vs participants; meals vs families | the same work counted once |
| Volunteers | 0.05 each | 0, shown as context | an input, not an outcome |
| Schools | 50 per school | 0.1 per m² of classroom space | IRIS+ unit |
| Scope | all 34 actions scored | 22 community actions scored; 12 that need capital, a licence or professionals kept aside (score 0) | the Community layer is for work volunteers can do |
| Price | IV × 0.5 in the sale currency | IV × rate × P × E in USD; E = 1.0 to 1.5 from declared costs | impact sets the price; costs only raise it |
| Resale | not specified | resale prices are set by the seller | the methodology prices the first sale only |

Paying for delivered impact rewards the creator after the fact: a purchase is not pre-funding or a donation.
