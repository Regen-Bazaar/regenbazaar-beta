## Notes and gaps

- **TH**: No 2026 notification replacing No. 14 was found in searches of mol.go.th, Bangkok Post and Nation; ThaiLawOnline (2026) says no change announced for 2026. Treat "no change since 2025-07-01" as verified only to the extent of those searches.
- **VN**: Decree text on the government portal not opened; figures are from two consistent law-firm/advisory sources.
- **ID**: Sources disagree on which province is lowest: Fortune IDN summary put Jawa Tengah at 2,317,386 (lowest), Pajakku lists Jawa Tengah at 2,327,386 and Jawa Barat 2,317,601 (lowest). Jawa Barat 2,317,601 used; difference is < 0.5%. Governor decrees not opened.
- **PH**: Lowest overall is 401 PHP (agriculture, BARMM outside the three cities); 411 is the lowest non-agriculture rate. Other regions' lowest rates (GMA, May 2026) were all ≥ 440 non-BARMM, so BARMM is lowest. BARMM rises by 25 PHP on 2026-12-01.
- **KH**: Cambodia has no general statutory minimum wage; the 210 USD figure is the garment/footwear/bags sector rate only. Hours conversion assumes 48 h/week.
- **LA**: A revision was under review (National Labour Committee, 2026-03-19: government proposal 2,710,451 + 900,000 allowance; unions 4,100,000). No approved new rate found as of 2026-09-28; the 2,500,000 figure may be superseded soon. Status of a 2026 increase: UNVERIFIED.
- **IN**: The 178 INR/day NFLMW is advisory and not binding; no binding national floor wage has been notified under the Code on Wages (in force 2025-11-21 per secondary sources). No official government page stating 178 was opened (the official ies.gov.in page is outdated at 160 INR, 2015). Mark 178 as UNVERIFIED against an official source. Real state minimums are much higher.
- **RU**: 164.33 h/month is the 2026 annual norm / 12; using 40 h × 52.14 / 12 = 173.8 h gives 155.9 RUB/h instead.
- **MY**: Hourly 8.72 is the order's own figure (1,700 / 195 h). Using 48 h/week × 52 / 12 = 208 h gives 8.17 MYR.
- Hours bases for ID, PH, KH, LA, IN are standard statutory references not re-opened in this session.
